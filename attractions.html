Boyertown History of Historical Vehicles

The Boyertown History of Historical Vehicles museum showcases the evolution of transportation with a focus on locally manufactured goods. This museum includes a diverse collection of over 90 Pennsylvania-built vehicles on display, including horse-drawn carriages, bicycles, motorcycles, cars, and trucks.

Colebrookdale Railroad

This railroad system is 8.6 miles long and offers a scenic view of Pennsylvania's "Secret Valley." The attraction uses restored vintage cars and themed decoration to make the tour more visually appealing.

Boyertown Area High School

The Boyertown Area High School is more than just a place to learn, it serves as a general hub of activity for the community. Many activities and events are held there, such as summer camps, and chorus. This also includes charities, theater production, and much more!

Boyertown Community Park

This park is nice area to spend the evening after working around the bustling stores in Boyertown. It has a diverse range of activities you can use to entertain yourself. This includes basketball, tennis, pickleball, and more. You could also go on a picnic, or take a little jog on the famous "Story Walk."

Historic State Theatre of Boyertown

The State Theatre of Boyertown was made in 1912 as the Lyrics Theater. It was run by a non-profit organization, so all their help came from volunteers and donations. It used to feature first-run shows, plays, and live events with affordable tickets. Today, The State Theature still does all of this, and more! .

Studio B Art Gallery

The Studio B Art Gallery is a volunteer-run center for viusal and literary arts. Founded by the Arts & Actvities Alliances in 2008, it offers classes, workshops, community events, and monthly themed exhibits. This building's mission is to celebrate creativity, learning, and personal involvement in visual & communication arts.
